Impact Investing Fundamentals

By Daniel Denisovich•December 12, 2025

Impact funds invest with the explicit intention of generating measurable positive social and environmental outcomes alongside financial returns. They target specific UN Sustainable Development Goals and report on their impact.

Impact investing focuses on creating tangible change in areas like clean energy, affordable housing, healthcare access, and sustainable agriculture.
