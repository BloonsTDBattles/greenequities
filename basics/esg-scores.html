How ESG Scores Work

By Daniel Denisovich•December 18, 2025

ESG scores rate companies on their sustainability performance using numerical ratings or letter grades. Rating agencies analyze company data, disclosures, and third-party reports to evaluate environmental impact, social responsibility, and governance quality.

Higher scores indicate better ESG practices and often correlate with lower long-term risk, helping investors make more informed decisions about which companies align with their values and financial goals.
