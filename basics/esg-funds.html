ESG Funds Explained

By Daniel Denisovich•December 16, 2025

ESG funds integrate environmental, social, and governance factors into investment decisions alongside traditional financial analysis. They screen out companies with poor ESG performance while focusing on those with strong sustainability practices.

Available as mutual funds, ETFs, and index funds, ESG funds help investors balance financial returns with positive environmental and social impact.
