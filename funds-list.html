Green Funds List

Clean Energy ETFs

iShares Global Clean Energy ETF ICLN

Includes: Solar, wind, hydro, and biofuel companies globally. Excludes: Fossil fuels, nuclear, and non-renewable utilities. Concentrated in ~100 pure-play clean energy stocks.

High Risk
Invesco Solar ETF TAN

Includes: Only solar panel manufacturers, installers, and solar tech companies. Excludes: All other energy types including wind. Highly concentrated single-sector exposure.

High Risk
First Trust NASDAQ Clean Edge Green Energy QCLN

Includes: U.S. clean energy, EVs (Tesla ~8%), solar, wind, batteries. Excludes: Fossil fuels, traditional utilities. Has significant Tesla/EV exposure unlike other clean energy funds.

High Risk
Invesco WilderHill Clean Energy ETF PBW

Includes: Emerging clean energy tech, hydrogen, fuel cells, small-cap green companies. Excludes: Large-cap stocks, fossil fuels. Tilts toward speculative, smaller companies.

High Risk
ALPS Clean Energy ETF ACES

Includes: U.S. and Canadian solar, wind, EVs, storage, hydrogen. Excludes: International stocks, fossil fuels. North America focused with broad clean energy mix.

High Risk
First Trust Global Wind Energy ETF FAN

Includes: Only wind turbine makers, wind farm operators, and wind component suppliers. Excludes: Solar, hydro, all other energy. Single-sector concentration in wind industry.

High Risk

ESG Broad Market ETFs

iShares ESG Aware MSCI USA ETF ESGU

Includes: Large/mid-cap U.S. stocks with above-average ESG scores. Excludes: Weapons, tobacco, thermal coal. Still holds oil companies with decent ESG scores. Light screening.

Medium Risk
Vanguard ESG U.S. Stock ETF ESGV

Includes: ~1,500 U.S. stocks passing ESG screens. Excludes: Weapons, tobacco, fossil fuels, nuclear, gambling, adult entertainment. Stricter exclusions than most ESG funds.

Medium Risk
SPDR S&P 500 ESG ETF EFIV

Includes: ~300 S&P 500 stocks with best ESG scores per sector. Excludes: Tobacco, weapons, coal. May still include oil majors. Maintains sector balance similar to S&P 500.

Medium Risk
iShares MSCI USA ESG Select ETF SUSA

Includes: ~180 U.S. companies with top ESG ratings in their sector. Excludes: Tobacco, alcohol, weapons, gambling, nuclear, fossil fuels. Stricter than ESGU with fewer holdings.

Medium Risk
iShares ESG MSCI USA Leaders ETF SUSL

Includes: Top 50% ESG-rated companies in each sector. Excludes: Tobacco, weapons, coal. Keeps sector diversification while selecting ESG leaders within each industry.

Medium Risk
iShares MSCI KLD 400 Social ETF DSI

Includes: 400 U.S. companies with strong ESG profiles. Excludes: Alcohol, tobacco, gambling, weapons, nuclear, GMOs. One of the oldest and strictest ESG indexes.

Medium Risk

Climate & Low Carbon ETFs

iShares MSCI ACWI Low Carbon Target ETF CRBN

Includes: Global stocks reweighted to minimize carbon exposure. Excludes: Nothing explicitly, but underweights high emitters. Tracks broad market with lower carbon footprint.

Medium Risk
VanEck Low Carbon Energy ETF SMOG

Includes: Alternative energy producers, energy efficiency companies. Excludes: Fossil fuels, traditional utilities. Focused on companies reducing carbon emissions.

High Risk
Global X CleanTech ETF CTEC

Includes: Clean energy, EVs, batteries, green buildings, pollution control. Excludes: Fossil fuels. Broader than pure clean energy funds with diverse green technology exposure.

High Risk

Sustainable Mutual Funds

Parnassus Core Equity Fund PRBLX

Includes: ~40 large-cap U.S. stocks with strong ESG and moats. Excludes: Weapons, tobacco, alcohol, fossil fuels, gambling. Actively managed with concentrated positions.

Medium Risk
TIAA-CREF Social Choice Equity Fund TICRX

Includes: U.S. large-caps meeting labor and environmental standards. Excludes: Weapons, tobacco, nuclear, gambling. Broad diversification with social screening.

Medium Risk
Calvert Equity Fund CSIEX

Includes: Large-cap ESG leaders with financial strength. Excludes: Weapons, tobacco, fossil fuels, private prisons. One of oldest responsible investing funds since 1987.

Medium Risk
Parnassus Mid Cap Fund PARWX

Includes: Mid-cap U.S. companies with quality management. Excludes: Weapons, tobacco, alcohol, fossil fuels. More volatile than large-cap but higher growth potential.

Medium Risk
Amana Growth Fund AMAGX

Includes: Sharia-compliant growth stocks with low debt. Excludes: Alcohol, tobacco, gambling, banks, insurance, pork. No interest income, avoids highly leveraged companies.

Medium Risk

Water & Natural Resources

Invesco Water Resources ETF PHO

Includes: U.S. water utilities, treatment, filtration, and infrastructure companies. Excludes: Non-water industries. Concentrated in ~35 stocks, mostly U.S. focused.

High Risk
First Trust Water ETF FIW

Includes: Water utilities, pipes, pumps, treatment systems companies. Excludes: Non-water businesses. Equal-weighted with ~35 holdings across water value chain.

High Risk
Invesco S&P Global Water Index ETF CGW

Includes: 50 largest global water companies including utilities and equipment makers. Excludes: Non-water businesses. More diversified geographically than PHO or FIW.

High Risk
Global X Wind Energy ETF WNDY

Includes: Only wind turbine makers, wind farm developers, wind components. Excludes: Solar, other renewables. Pure-play wind exposure, highly concentrated.

High Risk

International ESG Funds

iShares ESG Aware MSCI EAFE ETF ESGD

Medium Risk
iShares ESG Aware MSCI EM ETF ESGE

Includes: Emerging market stocks (China, Taiwan, India) with ESG scores. Excludes: Tobacco, weapons, coal. Higher volatility from emerging market and currency exposure.

High Risk
Vanguard ESG International Stock ETF VSGX

Medium Risk
BlackRock World ex U.S. Carbon Transition Readiness ETF LCTD

Medium Risk
