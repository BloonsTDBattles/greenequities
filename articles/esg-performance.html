Do ESG Funds Outperform the Market?

By Daniel Denisovich • November 20, 2025
← Back to Articles

Recent studies show that ESG funds have matched or exceeded traditional market returns over the past decade. Companies with strong ESG practices tend to demonstrate better risk management and operational efficiency, which translates into solid financial performance. While individual fund results vary, the data increasingly challenges the myth that sustainable investing requires sacrificing returns.

During market downturns, ESG funds have shown greater resilience, experiencing smaller drawdowns and faster recoveries. This defensive characteristic, combined with exposure to growing sustainable sectors, positions ESG investments favorably for long-term investors seeking both financial returns and positive impact.
