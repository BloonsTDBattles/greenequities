ICE List

Companies providing services, technology, financing, or other support to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and related agencies

This list is compiled from public sources including government contract databases, SEC filings, and investigative reporting. Investors who prioritize social responsibility may wish to consider these relationships when making investment decisions.

Amazon

NASDAQ: AMZN

Amazon Web Services (AWS) GovCloud hosts databases and systems used by the Department of Homeland Security, Customs and Border Protection, ICE, and U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services to track, monitor, and deport immigrants.

Amazon is also partnering with Flock Safety and Axon to help law enforcement, including ICE, access Ring doorbell footage without warrants. Approximately 1 in 5 American households has a doorbell camera, and Ring is the largest seller of this technology.

Apple

NASDAQ: AAPL

Apple has removed multiple apps designed to help people protect themselves from ICE officers, including ICEBlock. There have also been reports of ICE recruitment ads running on Apple Podcasts.

Aramark

NYSE: ARMK

Aramark provides food, facility management, and uniform services. The company supplies food and other services to prisons and immigration detention facilities and uses prison labor.

AT&T

NYSE: T

AT&T provides communications services to U.S. immigration authorities. The company provides communications and maintenance services to DHS and its subagencies, including CBP and ICE. According to USAspending.gov, AT&T currently has multiple active contracts with ICE.

Avelo Airlines

Private

Avelo was the only scheduled passenger airline providing services as part of the ICE Air network, which also includes charter airlines GlobalX, Eastern Air Express, Omni Air International, World Atlantic Airlines, Kaiser Air, Gryphon, and GEO Transport.

Update: In January 2026, Avelo announced it would cut ties with ICE following public pressure campaigns.

Axon Enterprise

NASDAQ: AXON

Axon manufactures less-lethal weapons and surveillance technology used by police, prison, immigration, and military agencies in the U.S. and worldwide. Axon has contracts to provide body cameras and tasers to DHS and is partnering with Amazon to help law enforcement access Ring doorbell surveillance footage.

Bank of America

NYSE: BAC

Bank of America has a history of lending to private prison and ICE detention companies. Despite previously pledging to cut ties with the industry, the bank has signaled willingness to resume providing financial services to companies like CoreCivic and GEO Group.

Bank OZK

NASDAQ: OZK

Bank OZK was named in a July 2025 amendment to a credit agreement with ICE detention operator GEO Group, serving as joint lead arranger and joint bookrunner.

BlackRock

NYSE: BLK

BlackRock is one of the largest shareholders of both GEO Group and CoreCivic, the two biggest private prison and ICE detention facility operators.

Booz Allen Hamilton

NYSE: BAH

Booz Allen Hamilton developed cloud analytics services for U.S. immigration authorities to track and target immigrants. The company has a $51.9 million contract to support ICE's Repository for Analytics in a Virtualized Environment (RAVEn) data repository.

Cellebrite

NASDAQ: CLBT

Cellebrite is an Israeli digital intelligence firm that supplies police, prison, and immigration authorities with hacking technologies to extract data from mobile devices.

Citizens Financial Group

NYSE: CFG

Citizens Bank has played a key role in financing ICE prison companies CoreCivic and GEO Group, even as other major banks cut ties with the industry.

Comcast

NASDAQ: CMCSA

Comcast Government Services has multiple contracts to provide internet and cable services to ICE and CBP facilities.

CoreCivic

NYSE: CXW

CoreCivic is a private prison and immigration detention facility operator with a long history of allegations involving human rights abuses and unsafe conditions. More than 90% of its stock is held by institutional shareholders including Vanguard and BlackRock.

Dell Technologies

NYSE: DELL

In 2025, Dell Federal Systems received awards worth over $63 million to provide Microsoft licenses, cloud services, and equipment to ICE and CBP.

Equifax

NYSE: EFX

Equifax has a history of providing ICE with data that allows them to track and target immigrants for deportation. Its contract with ICE ended in September 2025.

FedEx

NYSE: FDX

FedEx has a $1 million contract to provide ICE with delivery services, awarded in 2021 and running through 2026.

First Horizon Bank

NYSE: FHN

First Horizon helps ICE detention operator CoreCivic access financing through underwriting agreements.

General Dynamics

NYSE: GD

General Dynamics manufactures weapons and surveillance technology used to monitor the U.S.-Mexico border and immigrant communities. In 2025, General Dynamics subsidiaries received ICE and CBP awards worth more than $38 million for computer programming, consulting, and ammunition.

GEO Group

NYSE: GEO

GEO Group is ICE's top contractor. The company operates private prisons and ICE detention facilities and, through subsidiaries GEO Transport and BI, Inc., provides transportation and electronic surveillance to immigration authorities.

Google / Alphabet

NASDAQ: GOOG

Google runs ads for the Department of Homeland Security, including ICE recruitment campaigns. YouTube, owned by Google, also runs these recruitment ads.

Hilton

NYSE: HLT

ICE officers have reportedly stayed at numerous Hilton properties. When an independent franchise operator in Minneapolis canceled reservations for federal agents, Hilton revoked its franchise license. Hilton brands include Waldorf Astoria, Conrad, DoubleTree, Hampton, Embassy Suites, and many others.

Home Depot

NYSE: HD

Home Depot is included due to its silence on ICE's reported use of its store parking lots as locations for immigrant apprehensions.

L3Harris

NYSE: LHX

L3Harris provides phone tracking devices and other equipment to U.S. immigration authorities. The company manufactures cell-site simulators (Stingrays), and there are reports suggesting ICE may be using such devices to collect information near protests.

Leidos

NYSE: LDOS

Leidos provides imaging technologies and biometric systems to U.S. immigration authorities for border monitoring and surveillance.

RELX (LexisNexis)

NYSE: RELX

RELX's subsidiary LexisNexis provides U.S. immigration authorities with systems used to track and target immigrant communities and sells legal databases to prisons and jails.

Microsoft

NASDAQ: MSFT

LinkedIn, owned by Microsoft, runs ads for DHS including ICE recruitment campaigns. Microsoft also profits from ICE contracts with third parties including Dell, which provides Microsoft licenses to ICE and CBP.

Meta

NASDAQ: META

Meta runs ads for the Department of Homeland Security, including ICE recruitment campaigns, on both Facebook and Instagram.

Motorola Solutions

NYSE: MSI

Motorola Solutions provides tactical communications equipment and license plate readers to ICE.

Palantir

NASDAQ: PLTR

Since 2013, Palantir has provided ICE with systems like FALCON and Investigative Case Management (ICM), which have been used in workplace raids, large-scale enforcement operations, and investigations involving asylum seekers. Palantir's pattern-finding capabilities have been central to ICE's most aggressive tactics.

Regions Bank

NYSE: RF

Regions Bank helps ICE detention operator GEO Group access financing through indenture agreements.

Salesforce

NYSE: CRM

Salesforce's systems power the Unified Immigration Portal, a database used by U.S. immigration enforcement agencies to monitor and surveil immigrants. Reports suggest Salesforce is seeking to deepen ties with ICE by providing AI solutions for recruitment and operating ICE's tip line.

Sirius XM (Pandora, Spotify)

NASDAQ: SIRI

Pandora has reportedly run ads for DHS and ICE recruitment campaigns. Spotify has also run these ads in the past.

Spotify

NYSE: SPOT

Spotify has reportedly run ads for DHS and ICE recruitment campaigns. While not currently running ads, the company has not pledged to refuse them in the future.

StoneX

NASDAQ: SNEX

StoneX helps ICE detention operator CoreCivic access financing through underwriting agreements.

Synovus

NYSE: SNV

Synovus helps ICE detention operator CoreCivic access financing through underwriting agreements.

Target Hospitality

NASDAQ: TH

Target Hospitality has been awarded contracts for modular housing to accommodate ICE's mass detention operations.

Texas Capital Bank

NASDAQ: TCBI

Texas Capital Bank helps ICE detention operator CoreCivic access financing through underwriting agreements.

Thomson Reuters

NYSE: TRI

Thomson Reuters provides systems and databases used by U.S. immigration authorities to target and track immigrants.

Vanguard

Private

Vanguard is one of the largest shareholders of both GEO Group and CoreCivic, the two biggest private prison and ICE detention facility operators.

Warner Bros. Discovery (HBO Max, Hulu)

NASDAQ: WBD

HBO Max has reportedly run ads for DHS and ICE recruitment campaigns. Hulu, owned by Disney (NYSE: DIS), has also reportedly run these ads.

Wells Fargo

NYSE: WFC

Despite pledging to cut ties with the private prison and ICE detention industry in 2019, Wells Fargo has reportedly softened its policy statements to allow financing for detention companies in some circumstances.
